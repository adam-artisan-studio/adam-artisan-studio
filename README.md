## 🚀 What I am building

**Artisan Studio** is an AI interactive platform & CLI for Laravel developers.

**SAMI** collaborates with you. Her name stands for Smart Artisan Memory Intelligence.

- 🔍 **Scan**: runs static analysis, security audits and convention checks across the whole project and groups what she finds by cause.
- 🛠️ **Fix**: fixes what has a safe, known answer, one group at a time, and asks you when it needs a judgment call.
- 🧱 **Build**: takes an idea and builds it step by step on its own branch, with a prototype you can see first.
- 📄 **Insights**: a report on your project, with a starting point ordered by where your time pays off most.
- 🏙️ **The map**: every file is a building, and its height is its open findings, so you can see where the problems are and watch them come down as SAMI fixes them.

My goal is simple: make development more enjoyable and interactive. That is why I created SAMI, and over time I want the platform to support developers earning from this too.

⚙️ **Studio CLI** brings SAMI into your terminal, including the one in your IDE. Follow every scan, fix and build as it happens, build with SAMI, and use your own AI. Under the hood it runs PHPStan, Pint, Rector and more, and applies the edits she sends back.

👉 https://github.com/artisan-studio-launchpad/studio-cli
